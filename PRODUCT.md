# Product

## Register

product

## Users
People who open a browser many times a day and want a calm, useful first screen instead of a blank tab or an attention-heavy feed. Their immediate tasks are searching, opening a familiar destination, and holding a small thought.

## Product Purpose
Morrow replaces the Chromium new-tab surface with a local-first home page that can be tailored to the user's search engine, favourite links, name, theme, and note. Optional chat connects to an Ollama endpoint on the user's computer. Success is a starting point that feels personal while remaining fast and out of the way.

## Brand Personality
Quiet, precise, considered. The experience should make beginning a task feel lighter, not more stimulating.

## Anti-references
Avoid news-feed density, dashboards full of widgets, loud gradient-heavy productivity templates, decorative animation, and imposed accounts or cloud services.

## Design Principles

- Make the next action immediately obvious.
- Treat personalization as a small, reversible local preference.
- Let whitespace do the grouping.
- Keep all controls familiar and keyboard reachable.
- Respect the user's sensory preferences.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Meet standard keyboard-navigation expectations, retain visible focus states, use readable contrast, and respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
